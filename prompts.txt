“Maa’s Invisible Shield”

Insight: Moms don’t just cool the room; they quietly protect their children from every harshness – including the heat.

Story:
A single, wordless visual. A modest living room in the afternoon, filled with soft, cool light from an ONIDA AC mounted above. In the center, a mother naps lightly on the sofa, still in her work clothes, clearly exhausted, one hand unconsciously resting on her young son’s back. The boy sleeps peacefully on her lap in his partly changed school uniform, textbook open beside them, a few crayon drawings scattered on the floor. A faint hint of sweat on the mother’s forehead shows she has just come in from the heat outside, but the child’s face looks completely relaxed and comfortable. A glass of water with condensation sits on the table, untouched, suggesting she rushed to settle her son first and only then rested. Small details complete the story: the school bag dropped near the door, shoes loosely left under the table, a light blanket pulled more over the child than herself – all showing that her first instinct is always his comfort.

Brand Link:
The ONIDA AC is positioned directly above the mother and son, its cool airflow subtly visible in the gentle sway of a sheer curtain and a slight lift at the edge of the blanket. The AC’s design is modern and premium, with a clear ONIDA logo, hinting at powerful yet caring cooling without cluttering the frame. The overall colour palette inside the room is soft and slightly bluish, visually echoing “Let there be chill” and reinforcing ONIDA as part of the mother’s quiet care routine – an invisible extension of the way she protects and soothes her child, even in her sleep.

End line:
“For every degree she takes on herself. Give her a chill that cares.”

Execution:

Film + Instagram Reels (Silent Visuals)

10–20 second silent film starting on the stillness of the living room: the hum of the ONIDA AC, the slow movement of the curtain, then a gentle push-in to the mother and son sleeping together on the sofa.

Variants: the mother carefully adjusting the AC remote before dozing off, pulling the blanket a little higher over her son, then relaxing only when she feels the cool air on him.

Only ambient sounds and gentle music; no dialogues, no VO – the serenity of the scene and the body language tell the entire story.

Static + Print + OOH

Hero print: a wide shot of the living room with the ONIDA AC at the top of the frame and the mother–son pair asleep on the sofa beneath it, forming a calm triangle of protection and comfort.

Minimal copy: logo, product name, and end line placed outside the main visual so the image alone communicates the emotion of a mother’s invisible shield of comfort.

TV :

Maa’s Saved Moments”

Insight: Moms don’t just limit screen time; they quietly curate what’s worth remembering.

Story:
A single frame, no dialogue. Evening in a modest Indian living room, lit mainly by the soft glow of an ONIDA TV. On the screen: an old home video paused – a younger version of the mother holding her toddler, both laughing in a sunlit park. In front of the TV now, the same mother, slightly older, sits on the sofa with her now 8–10-year-old child resting their head on her shoulder as they watch that memory. Her eyes are moist but peaceful, the child is hugging her arm, fully absorbed. In the background, the father stands near a shelf of framed photos and a Wi‑Fi router, holding the remote, having just queued up this video for them. Outside the window, neighbouring flats are visible: dark, with cold bluish TV light spilling out, while this home glows warm and golden. Toys on the floor, a half-finished school project, and a small “Happy Birthday” banner still hanging from the corner hint that this video is from an earlier birthday. Without any words, the image tells us that this ONIDA TV is where a mother stores and replays the family’s most precious moments, not just shows.

Brand Link:
The ONIDA TV is the emotional centre of the frame – crisp, realistic screen quality and an easy-to-read interface hint at smart features like recordings, playlists, and cloud albums, but are kept subtle. The focus remains on how the mother uses these features to silently preserve and replay memories for her child. The neighbours’ darker rooms visually nod to “neighbour’s envy, owner’s pride” by contrast, without writing it. ONIDA becomes the brand that understands that for moms, a TV is not a gadget, it’s a memory keeper.

End line:
“For every memory she saves quietly. Give her a screen that treasures it.”

Execution:

Film + Instagram Reels (Silent Visuals)

10–20 second silent films: only ambient sounds and music, no dialogues.

Slow push-in shot from the window (seeing neighbours’ dark rooms) into the warm ONIDA-lit living room, ending on the paused home video and the mother–child hug.

Variations with different families: single mom and teen, joint family with grandmother watching an old black-and-white wedding clip, all using ONIDA TV as the memory hub.

Static + Print + OOH

Key visual: the exact moment of mother and child watching their own past selves on the ONIDA TV, with clear logo on the TV frame and the end line placed outside the main image area.

Optional print layout with small “thumbnails” of other saved moments along the bottom edge of the TV frame – first day at school, first bicycle ride, Diwali sparklers – reinforcing the idea of a curated memory library.

Social + UGC

Invite users to post a photo of their family watching an old memory on their TV, with a prompt like “Show us a moment your screen has kept safe,” adapted for ONIDA handles and hashtags.

Best entries get turned into minimalist, text-light posters by the brand, maintaining the silent, visual storytelling style.
